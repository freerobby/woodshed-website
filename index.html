Practice

The room where you put in the work.

Scales, difficult sections, and the gruntwork that makes a piece take shape. Woodshed gets out of the way while you play, then catalogs your session so you can hear it later.

Write

The room where a song starts.

A line shows up. Name it, record takes, jot a quick note. Just enough structure to shape an idea into a song. Once it’s fleshed out, you can practice it like any other piece.

How it works

Helping you track and hear your progress.

1

Record

Tap once and play. No need to touch your phone again until you’re done playing.

2

Woodshed splits

Your session is segmented and fingerprinted; app suggests matches to earlier performances.

3

Listen & rate

Listen to and rate your performances on the five-level scale. Determine what you need to work on.

4

Hear the difference

Open a piece and listen across weeks as your ratings climb.

Learning → Rough → Steady → Solid → Performance-ready

Standout features

Built to help, not distract.

One-tap recording

Tap Record, and put the phone down until you’re done playing.

Automatic piece detection

Splits your session into the pieces and exercises you actually played. Tune detection to how you practice.

Centered on what you play

Your performances are fingerprinted to show progress across your repertoire — not lost in a pile of recordings.

Rate it. Hear the change.

Five levels: Learning, Rough, Steady, Solid, Performance-ready. Rate as you go, then listen back across weeks.

Ideas, takes, a best take

Name an idea, record takes, and keep your best version handy.

One library

All your work, organized together: ideas, pieces, ratings, tags, and notes.

Mic or MIDI

Acoustic instruments through the mic, digital keyboards over MIDI. Same recording, same library, same matching.

Your device, your data

Recordings and library live on your phone. Backups go to your personal iCloud.
