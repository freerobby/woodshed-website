Woodshed helps you practice more effectively,
and capture song ideas the moment you have them.

Two ways to work. One library.

Practice

The room where you put in the work.

Scales, difficult sections, and the gruntwork that makes a piece take shape. Woodshed gets out of the way while you play, then catalogs your session so you can hear it later.

Write

The room where a song starts.

A line shows up. Name it, record takes, jot a quick note. Just enough structure to shape an idea into a song. Once it’s fleshed out, you can practice it like any other piece.

How it works

When you practice

1

Record

Tap once and play. No need to touch your phone again until you’re done playing.

2

Woodshed splits

It cuts the session into what you played, and suggests which piece each bit was. You confirm.

3

Listen & rate

Listen to and rate your performances on the five-level scale. Determine what you need to work on.

4

Hear the difference

Open a piece and listen across weeks as your ratings climb.

Learning → Rough → Steady → Solid → Performance-ready

When you write

1

Name it

Give the idea a name.

2

Record takes

Same one-tap recording.

3

Keep a best take

The version you want to come back to or share.
