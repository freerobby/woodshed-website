Practice

Tap once. Then play.

Scales, difficult sections, and the gruntwork that makes a piece take shape. Tap once and play — no need to touch your phone again until you’re done. Woodshed gets out of the way, then splits the session into segments and suggests which pieces you played. You confirm later, whenever it’s convenient, listen back, and rate each pass: Learning, Rough, Steady, Ready, Mastered. Open a piece and hear the difference across weeks as those ratings climb.

Write

Where a song starts

An idea comes to mind. Record it, name it if you want, and refine it with more takes. Attach notes or lyrics. Keep a best take — the version you want to come back to or share. Just enough structure to shape an idea into a song. Not a DAW, and not a pile of voice memos.
