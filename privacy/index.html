Privacy Policy

Effective 9 October 2026. This page is the current policy. If we make a material change, we will update this page and the date above.

This Privacy Policy describes how Woodshed (the iOS app, and the website at getwoodshed.com) handles information. Woodshed is operated by Robby Grossman. Contact: robby@freerobby.com.

Summary

  • Practice sessions, library items, recordings, and songwriting notes are stored on your device. If you are signed into iCloud, Woodshed also keeps a copy in your iCloud unless you turn off automatic backup in Settings. No Woodshed account is required.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not share personal information for cross-context advertising.
  • Optional diagnostics are on by default and can be turned off in Settings. Crash reports and usage analytics are separate switches.
  • You can send us feedback from inside the app. A report includes what you type and what you choose to include, such as an email address, a screenshot on a problem report, or diagnostics. “Improve detection” also includes that session’s audio or MIDI and its segment timings.
  • Woodshed is not directed at children under 13.
  • The app and website are provided with no warranty. Liability is disclaimed to the maximum extent permitted by law.

Information that stays on your device

Woodshed is built so your music stays yours. Practice sessions, audio recordings, library items (pieces, ratings, tags, history), songwriting ideas, takes, notes, MIDI you import or record, analysis results, and related files are stored on your device. You do not create a Woodshed account. We do not operate a Woodshed login, and we do not receive your recordings or songwriting notes as a condition of using the app.

If this device is signed into iCloud, automatic backup is on by default. Woodshed keeps one copy of your library in your iCloud and updates it as you record, edit, and delete. You can turn this off in Settings, or back up or restore yourself. Restoring replaces the library on this device after you confirm. A device that has never backed up will not overwrite a copy already in iCloud until you choose to replace or restore. Turning automatic backup off stops further updates. A copy already in iCloud remains until you delete it, or a later backup drops files you permanently deleted. That copy is in your Apple account under Apple’s terms. We do not receive it.

Deleting an item in the app removes it on that device. Permanently deleting it removes it from the iCloud copy on the next backup. Deleting the app removes the local library. An iCloud copy, a device backup you control, or a file you exported or shared yourself can remain.

Information we may collect or receive

Your library stays on your device and, when backup is on, in your iCloud. Depending on how you use Woodshed and the website, we and our service providers may collect or receive:

  • Identifiers and technical data: IP address, approximate location derived from IP, device type and model, operating system and version, app version, language, time zone, and anonymous identifiers used for diagnostics.
  • Crash and diagnostic data, if Crash reports is on: crash reports, hang reports, performance metrics, device and app state around a failure, and a replay of the screen around an error. That replay is for debugging failures. Text and images in it are masked, and it can still show the layout of the screen. It is not a recording of your practice, and it is not product analytics.
  • Product events, if Usage analytics is on: which screens you open and a fixed set of actions, such as starting or finishing a recording, creating a piece or idea, setting a rating, importing, backing up or restoring, reviewing a session, searching the library by playing, or finishing onboarding. Events may include coarse ranges (for example how long a recording was, or how many library matches a search returned) and short labels (for example which rating you chose, or that a recording used the microphone). We do not send piece names, idea names, recordings, MIDI, search audio, or email addresses. We do not record every tap, we do not tie these events to your name or email, and we do not replay your screen for product analytics.
  • In-app feedback, when you send it: the text you write in “Report a problem”, “Share an idea”, or “Improve detection”; an email address if you choose to leave one (the app may remember it on your device for next time); on a problem report, a screenshot of the screen you were on if you leave that option on (the image can include titles and other text visible there); and diagnostics such as app version, device, the screen you were on, recording state, and a short summary of your sessions, unless you turn diagnostics off. “Improve detection” also includes that session’s segment timings — what was detected, what you kept, whether a segment was named, and its rating, not the piece or idea name — and a copy of the session’s audio or MIDI. You can send feedback with Crash reports turned off.
  • Website and email: this website does not ask for an email address. Pages load fonts from Google, so a visit sends your IP address and browser details to Google for that request. If you email us, we have your address and the message. If you previously submitted an email on getwoodshed.com to be notified at launch, we processed that address and related form details so we could email you about launch.
  • Permissions on your device: the app may request microphone access (to record, and to search your library by playing a few bars), notification permission (local alerts), and Bluetooth (to pair a MIDI keyboard). Recordings and MIDI stay on your device and in your iCloud backup. A library search is matched on your device; that audio is discarded and is not saved. Recordings reach us only if you include them in feedback you choose to send.

Service providers that may process these categories include Apple (the App Store, iCloud, and iOS), Sentry (crash reports), PostHog (usage analytics), GitHub (this website), Google (fonts on this website), and Formspree (launch-list email collected before the app was on the App Store). They handle that information under their own policies. Feedback you choose to send is received by us so we can operate and improve Woodshed.

Optional diagnostics (default on)

Settings contains two independent toggles. Both default to on. Turning either off is free and takes effect immediately.

  • Crash reports. Crashes, hangs, performance metrics, and a masked replay around errors. Not product analytics. Turning this off does not disable in-app feedback. Handled with Sentry.
  • Usage analytics. The product events described above. Handled with PostHog.

In-app feedback

Feedback is sent only when you choose to send it. We use it to fix problems, consider ideas, reply if you left an address, and improve Woodshed, including how it splits practice into segments. If you do not leave an email address, we cannot write back.

To ask about a report you sent, email robby@freerobby.com.

How we use information

We use information to operate Woodshed, fix crashes and hangs, understand which screens and actions are used so we can improve the product, act on feedback you send from the app and reply to it if you gave us an address, improve the product (including practice-segment detection) using sessions people send for that purpose, email people who asked to be notified at launch, respond if you contact us, and meet legal obligations. We do not use personal information to build a cross-app advertising profile, and we do not sell it.

Sale, sharing, and advertising

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not use your recordings, library, or songwriting notes for advertising. Providers named here receive information only to do the work described for them above.

Retention

Library data on your device lasts until you delete it or delete the app. An iCloud copy lasts until a later backup removes what you permanently deleted, or you delete that copy. Device backups and files you exported stay under your control. Diagnostics and feedback, including any email address or session you included, are kept for as long as we and our providers need them to operate and improve Woodshed. We no longer collect email addresses on the website. Launch-list email already collected is kept until you ask us to remove it or we delete the list. Email you send us is kept as long as needed to respond and for ordinary records.

Children

Woodshed is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact robby@freerobby.com and we will delete it from systems we control.

Your choices

  • Turn off Crash reports, Usage analytics, or automatic iCloud backup in Settings. Each control is independent. Turning off backup does not delete a copy already in iCloud.
  • In the feedback composer, leave the email field empty, and turn off diagnostics, if you do not want them included. On a problem report you can also turn off the screenshot. “Improve detection” includes that session’s audio or MIDI and segment timings when you send it.
  • Delete local data in the app, or delete the app.
  • Email robby@freerobby.com to ask us to delete launch-list email, or to ask what feedback, diagnostics, or sessions we can locate and delete. We may not be able to remove an iCloud copy or a device backup you control.

Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical steps to protect information, including keeping your library on your device and in your iCloud, and limiting what we receive to the categories above. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. You are responsible for the physical security of your device and for any iCloud or export copies you make.

International processing

Woodshed is operated from the United States. Apple, Sentry, PostHog, GitHub, Google, and Formspree may process information in the United States and in other countries. If you use Woodshed from elsewhere, information may be processed outside your country, including where data-protection rules differ from yours.
